🔒 Architecture: Zero-Knowledge Client-Side

SmartLedger ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your financial privacy. SmartLedger is engineered with an offline-first, zero-knowledge architecture. This means your financial records are owned exclusively by you, stored securely on your device, and encrypted in a manner that ensures no one—including SmartLedger, its developers, or third-party cloud infrastructure—can decrypt or read your financial data.

This Privacy Policy transparently describes what information SmartLedger processes, our cryptographic design, how sensitive permissions (like SMS detection) operate strictly on your device, and your rights.

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture: We Cannot Decrypt Your Data

SmartLedger is built from the ground up on the principle of Zero-Knowledge Financial Privacy:

🔐 Cryptographic Guarantee
Because encryption and decryption occur solely in device memory using your private PIN, no one other than you can decrypt your data. There are no developer backdoors, master recovery keys, or remote access methods.
  • Client-Side End-to-End Encryption: All financial records, accounts, wallets, debts, budgets, notes, and pending expenses are encrypted using AES-256-GCM (Authenticated Encryption with Galois/Counter Mode) before being written to disk or sent to optional cloud sync.
  • Key Derivation on Device: Your encryption key is derived locally using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and 80,000 iterations, salted with a unique 16-byte cryptographically secure random salt.
  • No Server-Side PIN Storage: Your Master PIN and encryption keys are never transmitted, stored, or logged on any server or database.
  • Zero Decryption Capability: If our databases or your backup files were ever intercepted, they would appear only as scrambled ciphertext blobs.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

A. Financial Records (Encrypted Locally)

What you enter: Transactions (amounts, categories, payment modes, descriptions, dates), custom wallets/accounts, budgets, recurring expenses, loan/debt ledgers, savings goals, and vault notes.

How it is processed: Stored strictly in an encrypted local database on your device. Decrypted in memory only while the app is unlocked with your correct Master PIN.

B. SMS & Payment Alert Processing (100% On-Device)

SmartLedger includes an optional automated expense detection feature that can read transactional alerts from banks and payment apps (e.g., UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, net banking):

📬 100% Local On-Device Processing
Raw SMS messages and verification texts are never uploaded to any server, cloud provider, or third party. Personal texts and security OTPs are automatically ignored.
  • Strictly Financial Detection: The app utilizes Android's RECEIVE_SMS and READ_SMS permissions solely to scan for incoming bank debit/credit notifications and UPI reference alerts.
  • Personal Messages & OTPs Excluded: Personal text conversations, OTPs (One-Time Passwords), sensitive security codes, and non-financial SMS messages are systematically filtered out and ignored.
  • Review & Approval Inbox: Detected payment alerts are placed into your local "Pending Approvals" inbox. No expense is added to your permanent ledger without your explicit verification or approval.
  • User Control: SMS auto-detection is disabled by default and can be toggled on or off at any time in Settings → Auto-Detect Payment SMS. You can also revoke the permission in Android system settings at any time.

C. Account & Authentication Information (Optional Cloud Sync)

If you choose to enable cloud backup or multi-device sync, SmartLedger uses Firebase Phone Authentication or Google Sign-In to verify your identity. This processes your phone number or Google account ID and Firebase session tokens to associate your encrypted sync container with your account.

Crucially: Even when cloud synchronization is active, only encrypted ciphertext payloads are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Firestore. Firebase and Google cannot read your ledger contents.

D. Google Drive Backups (Optional)

If you use the Google Drive backup feature, SmartLedger writes an encrypted backup archive directly into your personal Google Drive storage. SmartLedger developers have zero access to your Google account, password, or Google Drive files.

E. Subscription & In-App Purchases

We use RevenueCat and Google Play Billing to manage Pro subscription entitlements and lifetime purchases. RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase tokens, app-store transaction IDs, and product identifiers to grant your entitlement. We never receive or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details.

F. Notifications & Reminders

If enabled, the app schedules local notifications for budget alerts, bill reminders, and detected payment expenses. Notification scheduling is handled locally on your device via Notifee.

G. Biometric Authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock)

If you enable Biometric Unlock, your Master PIN is stored locally in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / Keychain). Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device operating system. SmartLedger never accesses, reads, or stores your raw biometric data.

H. Optional Floating Quick-Log Bubble

SmartLedger includes an optional floating overlay bubble (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) allowing you to quickly log an expense from anywhere on your device. This feature only displays a quick-input prompt and does not monitor, capture, or record your screen, other applications, or external device activity.

5. Decoy Mode for Physical Privacy

SmartLedger includes an advanced Decoy Mode feature designed for personal safety and privacy under coercion:

  • If you configure a separate Decoy PIN, entering that PIN unlocks a completely independent, isolated decoy ledger.
  • Real financial databases and decoy databases are partitioned and encrypted separately. Accessing the decoy environment reveals zero traces of your authentic ledger.

6. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

  • Full Local Data Control: You can wipe all local financial records, cache, keys, and settings instantly from the app at Settings → Clear All Local Data.
  • Data Portability: You can export your financial data at any time in an encrypted portable JSON format or generate local PDF summary reports.
  • Cloud Deletion: If you have enabled cloud sync and wish to permanently delete your Firebase account and cloud-stored encrypted backups, you can do so in the app settings or by contacting us at the email below. Your cloud record will be permanently purged.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling SmartLedger completely removes the local database and cryptographic keys from your device.
